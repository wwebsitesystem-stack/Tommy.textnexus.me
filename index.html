01 / Release archive 13 entries // 2013—2023

Thirteen versions. Ten years.

Every main version since launch, oldest first. Select a card for release notes and a playable course. Dates are the original release dates; Steam and mobile builds occasionally shipped a day apart.

2013 — 2014

The 1.x era

Ten versions in fifteen months
1.0 Aug 13, 2013

The original release

The launch build: seven levels, two forms and the first level editor. Every version below started here.

  • Levels Stereo Madness → Jumper (7)
  • Forms Cube and ship
  • Editor Built-in editor and online levels
LaunchCubeShip
1.1 Sep 14, 2013

Time Machine & mirror mode

The first major update added Time Machine and flipped the screen with the mirror portal. Players could also comment on and dislike user levels.

  • Level Time Machine
  • Mechanic Mirror portal
  • Social Comments and dislikes
MirrorComments
1.2 Oct 21, 2013

Cycles & the ball

The ball form arrived, adding a gravity-swapping rhythm to the game, along with a new level and two cubes for the icon kit.

  • Level Cycles
  • Form Ball
  • Icons 2 new cubes
BallIcons
1.3 Nov 20, 2013

xStep & blue orbs

Blue jump orbs and pads gave creators a new way to chain jumps mid-air. xStep showed off what the editor could do.

  • Level xStep
  • Objects Blue orb and blue pad
  • Editor New decorations and effects
OrbsPads
1.4 Nov 26, 2013

Clutterfunk & size portals

Mini and big portals let players shrink or grow mid-level for the first time, and ship icons gained proper two-tone colouring.

  • Level Clutterfunk
  • Mechanic Mini and big portals
  • Icons 4 new ships
Mini modeShips
1.5 Jan 30, 2014

Theory of Everything & the UFO

The UFO form introduced tap-to-flap flying, alongside magenta pads and rings. User levels could be rated Demon for the first time.

  • Level Theory of Everything
  • Form UFO
  • Objects Magenta pad and ring
UFODemon rating
1.6 Mar 25, 2014

Electroman Adventures & Clubstep

Secret coins were added to every level and Clubstep became the first official Demon. Map packs and destructible blocks rounded out the update.

  • Levels Electroman Adventures, Clubstep
  • Collectibles 3 coins per level
  • New Map packs and Demon difficulty
Secret coinsMap packsDemon
1.7 May 22, 2014

Electrodynamix & speed changes

Speed portals brought slow, normal, fast and very fast gameplay, plus new backgrounds, a rainbow trail and glow effects.

  • Level Electrodynamix
  • Mechanic Speed portals
  • Cosmetics Rainbow trail and glow
SpeedGlow
1.8 Aug 7, 2014

Hexagon Force & dual mode

Dual portals put two icons on screen at once, while slopes changed how levels could be built. Hexagon Force tied it all together.

  • Level Hexagon Force
  • Mechanic Dual mode
  • Editor Slopes
DualSlopes
1.9 Nov 9, 2014

Blast Processing & Theory of Everything 2

The wave form arrived with two new levels. Editor layers and a 20,000-object limit landed, and the game reached Steam on 22 December 2014.

  • Levels Blast Processing, Theory of Everything 2
  • Form Wave
  • Platform Steam release (Dec 22, 2014)
WaveEditor layersSteam
2015 — 2023

The 2.x era

Three updates, eight years
2.0 Aug 26, 2015

Geometrical Dominator & Deadlocked

The robot form landed with two levels, user coins and moving objects. Accounts gained friends, following and messaging, plus the first secret vault.

  • Levels Geometrical Dominator, Deadlocked
  • Form Robot
  • New User coins and moving objects
RobotUser coinsVault
2.1 Jan 2017

Fingerdash & the spider

The spider form, dash orbs and custom rings arrived, alongside diamonds, the diamond shop and a round of editor improvements.

  • Level Fingerdash
  • Form Spider
  • New Dash orbs, custom rings, diamonds
SpiderOrbsDiamonds
2.2 Dec 19, 2023 Latest

Dash, the swing & Editor 2.0

The biggest update yet: the swing form, Dash, 86 new editor triggers, free-camera controls, platformer mode and Lists. It landed on Steam on 19 December 2023 and on mobile the next day.

  • Level Dash
  • Form Swing
  • Editor 86 triggers and camera controls
  • New Platformer mode and Lists
SwingEditor 2.0PlatformerLists
