01 / Release notes 1.3 // 2013

What 1.3 changed.

Blue orbs and pads gave creators a way to chain jumps in mid-air instead of relying on the ground.

  • xStep added as the tenth official level.
  • Blue jump orbs and blue pads added to the editor.
  • New decorations and effects for user levels.
